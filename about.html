About ToolSangam

ToolSangam is a collection of super simple, fast and free online tools made for everyone.

Hi, I am Abhinav from Jaipur, Rajasthan. I started ToolSangam with a simple idea - tools should be fast, without ads overload and without asking for signup.

Our mission is to provide 100+ useful tools like PDF tools, Image tools, Text tools, Calculators and more in one place.

All tools work in your browser, we don't store your files. Your privacy is our priority.
