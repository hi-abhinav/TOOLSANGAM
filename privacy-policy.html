Privacy Policy for ToolSangam

Last updated: Sep 10, 2026

At ToolSangam, accessible from toolsangam.online, we respect your privacy.

1. No File Storage

All our tools (PDF, Image, Text) run client-side in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to our server.

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google may use cookies and web beacons. You can opt-out via Google Ad Settings. We also use Cloudflare for security.

3. Log Data

We collect standard log data like IP, browser type via Cloudflare analytics for security.

If you have questions: contact@toolsangam.online
